Opponent-adjusted power

Raw is the default. Every final goal margin counts in full. Ratings jointly minimize one half the sum of squared errors between each home-minus-away final margin and rating_home minus rating_away, plus 3/2 times the sum of squared team ratings.

Equivalently each rating is (total scoring margin + opponents’ ratings summed once per meeting) / (games played + 3). Solve all ratings together. Ridge 3 shrinks ratings toward zero, not the raw game margins. No Huber loss, outlier damping, estimated home advantage or automatic blowout cap.

Selecting the optional 3-goal cap clips each game’s target margin to ±3 before the same fit. W–L–T does not rank or break ties. Zero-game teams remain unrated. Equal power rounded to eight decimal places uses competition ties.

Disconnected result groups are centered at zero by assumption, not measured equal strength. Akers and Boxx comparisons are provisional. Power ratings are separate from the goal forecasts, which use a three-game prior and broad uncalibrated uncertainty.

Canonical engine: opponent-ridge-margin-v3, exported unchanged from the shared source. No new result collection or historical refit happens in this site’s exporter.
